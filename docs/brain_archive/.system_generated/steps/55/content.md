# Lofi Video Generator
Keywords "sunset", "morning" or "night" pick the color scheme and chords.
Only use audio you have rights to. The GIF must be a direct link to a .gif file, under 20 MB.
Each line gets an equal share of the length and is shown over a generated lofi track.
Removes silent pauses longer than about 0.6 s from the video currently shown below.
